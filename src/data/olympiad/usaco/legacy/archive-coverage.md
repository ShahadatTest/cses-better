## Audit boundary and gaps

The current USACO catalog covers 2014–2025; this supplement extends it back through the oldest archive lists located in the site's current `page=<round>problems` archive pattern. Searches for the same `results`/`problems` naming pattern for 2005–2010 and January/February/Open 2011 did not yield usable official round archives. This is a search result, not proof those contests never existed. The 2026 round URL patterns tested (`jan26`, `feb26`, `mar26`, `usopen26`, with both `results` and `problems` suffixes) fell back to generic site content and had no identifiable contest listing at audit time. Recheck the official archive before claiming those years are complete.

The archive index contains metadata only; it does not assert that statement text or sample data may be redistributed. Every record should receive its own reviewed visual explanation before it is exposed as a complete learning entry.
